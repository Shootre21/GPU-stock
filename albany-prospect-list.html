Albany, Oregon Prospect List

A clean call-first prospecting sheet for offering practical cybersecurity services to small businesses in Albany. This version prioritizes organizations that likely handle customer data, rely heavily on email and web systems, and may not have in-house security support.

Prepared for Cinderstrike Local outreach focus Phone-first prospecting Healthcare / real estate / professional services

Best Targets

Healthcare, dental, vision, real estate, law, accounting, and insurance are the strongest categories because they often have sensitive data, heavy email workflows, and limited in-house security staffing.

Best Opening

Lead with a practical risk conversation, not “do you want cybersecurity.” Ask who handles IT or security decisions for the office and keep it short.

Best First 5

River View Family Eyecare, Cedarwood Dental Professionals, Valley Clinics, Cadwell Realty Group, and Albany Vision Therapy are the strongest first-pass calls.
