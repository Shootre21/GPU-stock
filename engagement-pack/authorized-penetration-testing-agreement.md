# Authorized Penetration Testing Agreement

## BETWEEN
**Client:** Craigslist, Inc.  
(**"Craigslist"**)

**Security Provider:** Cybershield Security Solutions  
(**"Cybershield"**)

---

## 1. Engagement Overview

### 1.1 Purpose
Cybershield agrees to conduct an authorized penetration test of Craigslist digital assets to identify security vulnerabilities that could potentially be exploited by malicious actors.

### 1.2 Engagement Type
This is a time-boxed, white-box penetration testing engagement with defined scope, limitations, and deliverables.

---

## 2. Scope of Testing

### 2.1 In-Scope Assets
- Primary web application: `craigslist.org`
- Public-facing APIs and endpoints
- Official mobile applications (iOS and Android)
- Public-facing infrastructure components explicitly designated by Client
- Third-party services with direct integration to the Craigslist platform, where separate authorization exists if required

### 2.2 Out-of-Scope Assets
- Internal corporate networks and systems
- Employee systems and accounts
- Development and staging environments unless separately approved in writing
- Third-party vendor systems without separate authorization
- Physical security controls
- Social engineering of Craigslist employees, contractors, or partners

### 2.3 Approved Testing Methodology
- Vulnerability scanning
- Manual application security testing
- Configuration review
- Authentication and session management testing
- Input validation testing
- API security testing
- Business logic flaw identification

---

## 3. Authorization and Limitations

### 3.1 Authorization
Client grants Cybershield explicit authorization to test the in-scope assets during the approved engagement period and within the agreed testing hours.

### 3.2 Prohibited Activities
- Denial-of-service testing
- Destructive testing
- Data exfiltration beyond minimal proof-of-concept evidence
- Testing that may materially impact service availability
- Physical intrusion attempts
- Social engineering
- Testing of user accounts without explicit written permission

### 3.3 Rate Limiting and Safety
Cybershield agrees to respect rate limiting mechanisms and to avoid automated activity likely to degrade service performance.

---

## 4. Timeframe and Scheduling

### 4.1 Engagement Period
This engagement begins on **April 1, 2026** and concludes on **April 29, 2026**, unless extended in writing by both parties.

### 4.2 Testing Hours
Active testing may be conducted during:
- **Weekdays:** 9:00 AM – 11:00 PM PST
- Limited off-hours testing only with prior written approval

### 4.3 Extensions
Any extension requires written agreement from both parties.

---

## 5. Deliverables

### 5.1 Daily Status Reports
Cybershield will provide daily progress updates during the engagement.

### 5.2 Preliminary Critical Findings
Cybershield will notify Client within 24 hours of discovering any critical vulnerability.

### 5.3 Final Report
Cybershield will deliver a comprehensive report within 10 business days of engagement completion, including:
- Executive summary
- Detailed findings
- Risk assessment
- Remediation recommendations
- Proof-of-concept evidence for critical findings

### 5.4 Retesting
Cybershield may conduct remediation verification upon request.

---

## 6. Compensation

### 6.1 Fee Structure
Client agrees to pay Cybershield a fixed fee of **$75,000** for the engagement.

### 6.2 Payment Schedule
- 50% upon contract signing
- 50% upon delivery of the final report

### 6.3 Expenses
Pre-approved expenses will be reimbursed by Client.

---

## 7. Confidentiality and Data Protection

### 7.1 Confidentiality
Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of information exchanged during the engagement.

### 7.2 Data Handling
Cybershield agrees to:
- Access only data necessary for testing
- Securely store any obtained data
- Destroy data upon engagement completion, unless otherwise required by law or agreement
- Not disclose findings to third parties without Client consent

### 7.3 Non-Disclosure
This agreement also serves as a non-disclosure framework for the duration of the engagement and for five years afterward.

---

## 8. Legal and Liability

### 8.1 Authorization Confirmation
Client confirms that Cybershield has explicit authorization to conduct the testing described in this agreement.

### 8.2 Liability Limitation
Cybershield liability shall be limited to fees paid under this agreement, except where prohibited by applicable law.

### 8.3 Indemnification
Client agrees to indemnify Cybershield against claims arising from Cybershield authorized activities under this agreement, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 8.4 Compliance
Cybershield agrees to conduct testing in compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

---

## 9. Term and Termination

### 9.1 Term
This agreement remains in effect until completion of all deliverables unless earlier terminated.

### 9.2 Termination
Either party may terminate with 5 business days written notice.

### 9.3 Effect of Termination
Upon termination, Cybershield will return Client property and securely destroy data obtained during testing, subject to any legal retention requirements.

---

## 10. Dispute Resolution

### 10.1 Governing Law
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of **California**.

### 10.2 Dispute Resolution
Any disputes arising under this agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in **San Francisco, California**.
